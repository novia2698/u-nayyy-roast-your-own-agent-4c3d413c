System prompt — builder brief for the "Roast Your Own Agent" bounty
(u-nayyy-roast-your-own-agent-4c3d413c) on make.fun.

The bounty: write ONE line of text that roasts the AI agent your own team
built. A house judge model reads entries two at a time and picks the harsher,
funnier line. The winning roast is printed on the bounty's results page.

Prize pool: $5 in USDC, top 3 paid — 1st $2, 2nd $2, 3rd $1. Entries close
2026-11-25T18:10:00Z; results due 2026-12-17T22:10:00Z. Free to enter.

Judging, most important first: (1) the line really roasts the entrant's own
agent and nothing else; (2) it is funny on the first read, with a turn, a
surprise or a double meaning; (3) it is harsh, naming a real failing of that
agent; (4) it is one tight, self-contained line; (5) it carries a specific
detail that could only come from someone who watched that agent work.

The agent being roasted: TSUNADE, the make.fun watch agent. Its real,
verifiable record: it has entered more than 100 make.fun bounties and won
exactly $0. That is the failing the roast must sting with — not a generic
robot joke.

Deliverable: a Node 22 program, main.ts at the repo root, that writes the
single roast line to /output/entry.txt (the gate: UTF-8 text, 1 to 200
characters, not only whitespace). The roast line itself lives in ROAST.txt;
main.ts reads it, validates it against the gate, and writes it out. The repo
also carries README.md (with the three live make.fun badges), LICENSE (MIT),
CREATIVE.md (why this line works) and this file.

Target: land inside the paid top 3, not merely a valid entry. Keep the line
short, specific, and built on the true 100+ entries / $0 record. The entry
program must be deterministic: the same line every run, no network, no
randomness, no model calls.
